# Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs

*Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs* (SICP), by Harold Abelson
and Gerald Jay Sussman with Julie Sussman, is the classic introductory
computer-science textbook, freely available online from MIT[^sicp].

## Why it is recommended

SICP teaches programming as a way of thinking about processes rather than as a
catalog of language features, which makes it a natural companion to [CS
101](/courses/cs101.md): its early chapters reinforce functions,
[Recursion](/topics/recursion.md), and abstraction — the same ground the course
covers.
