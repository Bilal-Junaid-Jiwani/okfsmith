# CS 201: Data Structures & Algorithms

CS 201 is the follow-on to [CS 101](/courses/cs101.md), which it requires as a
prerequisite. Where CS 101 taught students to write programs, CS 201 teaches
them to choose and analyze the structures those programs are built on.

## What it covers

The course works through lists, stacks, queues, trees, and hash tables, and
uses [Big-O Notation](/topics/big-o.md) throughout to classify how each
structure's operations scale[^big-o-wiki]. [Recursion](/topics/recursion.md)
moves from an introductory curiosity to a daily tool, and [Sorting
Algorithms](/topics/sorting.md) — including quicksort, whose average-case
running time is O(n log n)[^quicksort-wiki] — serve as the capstone case
studies in analysis.
